📋 LumenDesk 300 · assembly & setup

Read all instructions before beginning. This guide covers the complete assembly of the LumenDesk 300 task lamp and its wireless charging base.

  • Phillips PH1 screwdriver
  • Hex key (included)
  • Soft cloth
  • Stable work surface

🔧 Base & arm assembly

1 Place the weighted base (part A) on a flat, non-abrasive surface. Remove the protective film from the rubber foot ring.
2 Align the lower arm (part B) with the threaded hole in the base. Insert the M6×20 bolt and tighten by hand until snug. Use the included hex key for an additional quarter turn. Do not overtighten.
3 Slide the cable guide clip (part C) onto the lower arm at approximately the midpoint. Route the lamp cable through the clip and down toward the base.
4 Attach the upper arm (part D) to the lower arm using the pre-installed pivot screw. Adjust the tension with a 2.5 mm hex key if the arm does not hold position.

💡 Lamp head & diffuser

5 Remove the lamp head (part E) from its protective bag. Peel off the clear protective film from the diffuser lens.
6 Connect the lamp head to the upper arm: align the two small pins and press firmly until you hear a click. The head should rotate freely but not wobble.
7 Plug the USB-C connector into the port on the rear of the base. Connect the power adapter to a wall outlet. The LED indicator will blink blue once, then turn solid white.

🔋 Wireless charging pad

8 Peel the backing from the adhesive charging pad (part F) and affix it to the marked circle on the base. Press firmly for 10 seconds.
9 Place a compatible phone or earbuds case on the pad. A soft chime confirms charging has started. If no chime, adjust the device position.

⚙️ Final checks & care

10 Check all connections. Ensure the lamp head is securely seated. Wipe the lamp with a dry microfiber cloth. Avoid liquid cleaners on the diffuser.
